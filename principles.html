Principles of the Support Fund

Hope Association is a non-profit association based in Bern, Switzerland. Our support fund helps people in need to build a livelihood and stand on their own feet. These are the principles we work by.

🤝

1. Interest-free, without profit

We charge no interest, no fees and no commission. You return only the amount you received.

Hope Association does not make any profit from this fund. It is not a bank and not a lending business. Every amount that flows back goes straight into the fund and is used to help the next person in need. This is why we call the contributions you pay back a contribution to the fund rather than a repayment of a debt.

🫱

2. Who we support

The fund exists for people in need who have no access to fair and affordable credit.

Support is given on the basis of need alone. We support people regardless of religion, caste, ethnic background, gender or political conviction. Nobody has to belong to a church, a community or an organisation to receive help, and no participation of any kind is expected in return.

Members and board members of Hope Association receive no benefit from the fund.

🌱

3. What the fund is used for

Support is given for purposes that help a person or a family towards a self-sufficient and dignified life:

  • Income generation — a small trade, workshop or microenterprise that allows a family to earn its own living
  • A vehicle for income generation — for example a rickshaw or a cart used as a means of earning
  • Land for livelihood — a small plot used to grow food or generate income
  • Education — school and college fees, training and apprenticeships
  • Medical treatment — where treatment would otherwise be out of reach
  • Housing — deposits and basic building or repair work for a family's own home
  • Release from exploitative debt — freeing a family from money lenders charging ruinous interest
  • Social initiatives — projects that serve a community, such as care for children, the elderly or people with disabilities

What the fund is not used for:

  • Investment, speculation or the purchase of property as an asset
  • Businesses run purely for profit maximisation rather than a family's livelihood
  • Luxury spending, or purposes that create no lasting benefit
  • Political or commercial activities of any kind
📋

4. How decisions are made

Every request is reviewed by a local Hope coordinator, who knows the situation on the ground, and then assessed by the Hope Association team in Switzerland. We look at the need, at what the support would make possible, and at whether the plan is realistic.

We cannot help everyone. When the fund is fully committed, we pause new requests rather than make promises we cannot keep. A request that is not approved is not a judgement on the person.

💙

5. Contributions back to the fund

We agree together on a monthly amount that is realistic for your situation. There is no collateral, no penalty and no debt collection. We do not work with enforcement agents, and nothing is taken from anyone.

If illness, crop failure or another hardship makes contributions impossible for a time, tell your coordinator. We will pause or adjust the arrangement. In cases of serious hardship the remaining amount can be waived entirely.

We ask for honesty rather than guarantees. What comes back helps the next family.

🏛️

6. Accountability

Hope Association (Verein Hope Bern, Evangelisches Gemeinschaftswerk) is a non-profit association under Swiss law, based at Brunnmattstrasse 50, 3007 Bern. The board serves on a voluntary basis.

Every advance from the fund is recorded, and the fund is reported on in the association's annual accounts. Questions about the fund are welcome at any time.
